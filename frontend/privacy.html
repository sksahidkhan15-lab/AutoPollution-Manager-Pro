Customer Consent & Privacy Policy

Compliant with India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act & TRAI Regulations

1. Purpose of Data Collection

AutoPollution Manager Pro collects and processes motor vehicle registration identifiers, vehicle owner contact numbers, fuel classifications, and emission test expiry timestamps solely for delivering timely PUC certificate renewal reminders and facilitating statutory environmental compliance.

2. Explicit Customer Consent & Opt-Out

Prior to activating automated WhatsApp or SMS notifications, testing centres capture explicit customer consent. Vehicle owners possess the statutory right to opt-out of automated notifications at any time by requesting their testing centre or replying STOP to automated messages.

3. Tenant Separation & Security

Data stored by one testing centre is logically and cryptographically partitioned from all other testing centres using Firestore zero-trust security rules and role-based access control tokens. Under no circumstances is customer data sold, leased, or monetized for commercial advertising.

4. Meta WhatsApp Cloud API Privacy

When automated reminders are dispatched via the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API, communications strictly utilize pre-approved transactional utility templates compliant with Meta's Business Messaging Policies.
